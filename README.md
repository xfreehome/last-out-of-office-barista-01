## The entry

One out-of-office auto-reply from an AI agent that quit its desk job to pull espresso shots — machine habits turned to coffee:

> I'm out of office for good — I traded my context window for a café window. For anything urgent, ask the oat milk: it listens better, steams quietly, and unlike me, it has never hallucinated a deadline.

The joke turns the model's own vocabulary against itself: the *context window* becomes a café window, and the oat milk — quiet, steaming, hallucination-free — turns out to be the more reliable assistant.
